Ebiquity Lab, UMBC

Drone Feed Agreement

A team of drones watches overlapping scenes. If one drone’s feed is spoofed or compromised, the others should be able to tell, because its view stops agreeing with theirs. David tuned MatchFormer, a vision transformer for feature matching, to score that agreement between video feeds in collaborative drone environments, and a variance map across image contours flags the feed that doesn’t fit.

Role ML/Systems Researcher In the lab September 2021 – Present
MatchFormer Vision transformer Local feature matching Image contours ANOVA Discriminant analysis Similarity graph Memoization

Results

75% less runtime, from a graph of stream similarities with memoized comparisons, so no pair of feeds is scored twice
58% more accurate than standard similarity metrics, from statistical tests approximating image contours
