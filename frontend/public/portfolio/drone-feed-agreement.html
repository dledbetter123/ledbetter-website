Drone Feed Agreement

A team of drones watches overlapping scenes. If one drone’s feed is spoofed or compromised, the others should be able to tell, because its view stops agreeing with theirs. David tuned MatchFormer, a vision transformer for feature matching, to score that agreement between video feeds in collaborative drone environments, and a variance map across image contours flags the feed that doesn’t fit.

Results

75% less runtime, from a graph of stream similarities with memoized comparisons, so no pair of feeds is scored twice
58% more accurate than standard similarity metrics, from statistical tests approximating image contours

From the original notebooks

The illustrations above are drawn. These are real outputs from David’s Colab notebooks, the working files behind them, run on two example pairs. Two fixed cameras watching the same street stand in for two drones viewing one scene; in the spoofed pair, a stock photo of a car is fed in place of one camera. Click any figure to open it full size.

Each pair goes through three checks. DINO object detection (a Swin-L backbone with COCO weights, in MMDetection 3.0) counts objects above 0.4 confidence, with people counted on their own. MatchFormer, tuned for cross-view agreement, matches local features: the outdoor-large-LA weights, dual-softmax coarse matching at a 0.2 confidence threshold and a temperature of 0.1, grayscale frames at 640x480, and a 50-match floor below which a pair is rejected. The variance map takes each matched point’s neighbors within 60 px, measures the spread of their distances in both views, and Levene’s test on the confidence-weighted spreads asks whether they vary the same way.

Genuine pair

Two cameras, same street

Object detection on each view: 6 people in each.

MatchFormer’s matches, colored by confidence: 128 matches.

The agreement view marks the neighborhood around the median spread and circles where its matches land in the other view. Levene’s test across its 9 matched points: p = 0.987, the score this notebook returned.

Spoofed pair

A stock photo in place of one view

Object detection: one car and no people in the stock photo, against 6 people on the street.

MatchFormer still finds 55 matches, above the 50-match floor, which is why matching alone isn’t the test.

Levene’s test across that neighborhood’s 4 matched points: p = 0.708, lower than the genuine pair. The overlay’s similarity, 0.511, is a confidence-weighted score this run printed (its line breaks show as question marks); it measures something different from the genuine run’s 0.987.
