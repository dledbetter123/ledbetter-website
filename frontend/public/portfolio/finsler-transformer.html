The Finsler Transformer

What if attention were a curvature a sequence moves through? The Finsler Transformer replaces the transformer's O(T2) attention with geodesic flow on a learned Finsler manifold, where a sentence is a geodesic, aiming at O(T) autoregressive generation. The flow comes from the metric itself: the spray that drives it is derived analytically from the learned metric with autograd.

Why Finsler

Euclidean: symmetric

A standard transformer treats representation space as Euclidean. Distances are symmetric and direction-independent, so relevance from A to B and from B to A come from the same inner product. Language is directional: A implies B is a different claim from B implies A, and syntax constrains what may follow what.

Riemannian: still symmetric

A Riemannian metric gij(x) lets distance vary with position but keeps it symmetric in direction. Isotropic diffusion on such a manifold tends to over-smooth: tokens drift toward generic attractors and lose their identity, the rank-collapse pathology seen in deep graph networks.

Finsler: direction-dependent

A Finsler norm F(x, v) assigns a direction-dependent cost to motion at every point of the manifold, with F(x, v) ≠ F(x, −v) in general. Asymmetry, the property language needs most, is built into the metric.

The project uses the simplest non-Riemannian Finsler metric, the Randers metric:

F(x, y) = √(aij(x) yiyj) + bi(x) yi,  with ‖b‖a < 1

  • aij(x) is a learned Riemannian background: baseline semantic similarity.
  • bi(x) is a learned 1-form, which the repository calls a wind, that biases flow toward the next logical token.
  • Together they let the geometry stiffen to block information flow in irrelevant directions and loosen to channel it toward grammatically or semantically licensed continuations.

A sentence is a geodesic

The governing idea: context is the geometric deformation of the latent space, in place of an additive matrix operation.

Sequence as trajectory

A sentence is a path x(t) through the manifold. The exact order of tokens defines the differential equation whose solution is that path.

Attention is curvature

As the state moves from w1 to w2, the nonlinear connection distorts the tangent space seen by w3. The attention a token pays to its predecessors is encoded in the accumulated distortion along the route. No T×T score matrix is formed.

Path-dependent meaning is holonomy

Two routes that arrive at the same point carry different holonomy, the accumulated rotation of a frame transported along the path, and therefore different representations.

Generation integrates the geodesic equation:

d2xi/dt2 + 2Gi(x, ẋ) = 0

  • The spray coefficients Gi(x, y), the laws of motion of the sequence, are derived analytically from the Randers metric through ½F2 and the Euler–Lagrange equation, using autograd.
  • The nonlinear connection Nij = ∂Gi/∂yj drives frame parallel transport, Ht+1 = exp(−N dt) Ht, which is where holonomy accumulates.
  • Training minimizes geodesic deviation, the residual of the geodesic equation on observed sequences, plus a rollout term that forces the learned metric to produce stable trajectories under iteration as well as pointwise fits.

In the long-sequence regime, T much larger than d, where attention hurts most, geometric transport is asymptotically favorable.

What worked, and what failed

This is an exploration repository, and its failure analysis is the most valuable part of it.

Synthetic 2D: worked

Deterministic spirals

With high signal-to-noise, where acceleration is uniquely determined by position and velocity, the metric-first model learns a sensible geometry.

Synthetic 2D: worked

Stochastic drift

On constant-wind stochastic walks, geodesic deviation correctly plateaus at the noise floor: the Randers b-form learns the drift direction and the spray learns roughly flat space. Reproducing the mean trajectory is the right answer here.

Failure mode 1

A free-form MLP spray

Expressive but geometrically ungrounded: no guarantee of 2-homogeneity, metric compatibility or consistent geodesics.

Failure mode 2

Additive holonomy

Accumulating a vector onto position is a translation. The design needs a frame transformation, and the translation silently discards the direction-dependence the project exists for.

Failure mode 3

A space mismatch

Accelerations measured in raw data space, while the spray predicted them in encoded space, produced meaningless gradients.

Failure mode 4

Compounding layers

Compounding all geodesic layers on every step exploded rollout error far outside the training regime.

  • The lesson was the metric-first architecture: learn the Randers metric explicitly, derive the spray analytically from it, and train on geodesic deviation directly.
  • The current frontier is metric expressiveness. A smooth 2–3 layer metric network cannot carve the sharp, localized channels language needs. The proposed fix is a Hybrid Randers manifold: the analytic spray plus a residual network conditioned on the local metric parameters, so the network corrects the geometry and the geometric guarantees stay.

Status and next tests

Validation so far is on synthetic 2D dynamics. The repository lists these next tests:

  • Spatially varying test geometry: rotating-wind fields and multi-attractor dynamics. The direct test of whether position- and direction-dependent metrics learn trajectory structure.
  • A language-model proof of concept: train on tokenized text and compare perplexity against a parameter- and FLOP-matched transformer. Especially at long context, where the O(T) advantage is largest.
  • Multi-chart manifolds: overlapping local coordinate charts, each with its own metric, since a single global metric is too smooth for high-dimensional language.
  • Richer Finsler structure: general (α, β) metrics beyond Randers, connected to information geometry through Fisher metrics on the output simplex.

The sister project, Sparse Geometric Signal Transport, works in a spectral fiber bundle, with tokens as sparse Fourier constellations. The Finsler Transformer works on the base manifold itself: the metric is asymmetric, the sequence is the geodesic, and holonomy replaces the attention matrix.
